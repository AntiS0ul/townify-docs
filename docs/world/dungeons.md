# Dungeons & Combat

For players who want a fight: instanced dungeons with custom bosses, a PvP arena, custom enchantments, and a bounty system that puts a price on other players' heads.

## Dungeons

TownifyMC has a **dungeon hub** with several themed dungeons full of custom mobs and loot.

```
/warp dungeons      # go to the dungeon hub
/dungeonkeyshop     # buy dungeon entry keys
```

Each dungeon needs an **entry key**, bought with money from the key shop:

| Dungeon | Key price |
|---------|:---------:|
| :material-skull-outline: **Necromancer's Cave** | <span class="chip money">$25,000</span> |
| :material-treasure-chest: **Goblin Stash** | <span class="chip money">$25,000</span> |
| :material-castle: **Piglin Castle** | <span class="chip money">$50,000</span> |
| :material-weather-night: **Deep Dark** | <span class="chip money">$150,000</span> |
| :material-dragon: **End Calamity** | <span class="chip money">$200,000</span> |

!!! tip "Gear up first"
    The cheaper dungeons are the place to start. Grab the **Dungeon Kit** from `/kit` (costs $25,000, hourly cooldown), bring food and a full set of gear, and go with friends when you can.

## PvP Arena

Want to fight other players without a [siege](../towns/war.md)? The **PvP Arena** is the place for it.

```
/warp pvp
```

PvP outside the arena and sieges follows your town's and the world's PvP settings. Remember the [rules](../getting-started/rules.md): no trap-killing, TP-killing, or baiting players into fights.

## Custom enchantments

On top of vanilla enchants, TownifyMC has **custom enchantments** with extra effects for weapons, armor, and tools.

```
/ce             # open the custom enchantments menu
```

Browse the menu to see every custom enchant, what it does, and how to get it.

## Bounties

Anyone can put a **bounty** on another player. Kill someone with a bounty on them and you collect it.

```
/bounty         # open the bounty board
```

- Browse active bounties to find targets worth hunting.
- Place a bounty on someone who's wronged you (within the rules).
- There's also a **bounties** warp: `/warp bounties`.

!!! warning "Bounties don't override the rules"
    Hunting a bounty target is still normal PvP: no trapping, TP-killing, or griefing to get to them. Towny protection and PvP settings still apply.

## Muskets

The **Gun Merchant** sells a **Musket** (<span class="chip money">$245,000</span>) and **Musket Ammo** (<span class="chip money">$5,000</span>) for players who want something with more range.

```
/gunmerchant
```

## If you die

Your items go into a **grave** instead of spilling on the ground. Use `/graves` to see them, or pick **Your Gravestone** in the `/warp` menu to teleport straight back. See [Death Chests](../perks/cosmetics.md#death-chests-graves).
