# Server Rules

These rules apply to **every part of the Townify community** — in-game chat, private messages, Discord, builds, item names, signs, books, maps, shops, auctions, tickets, and any other server-connected space. Staff may update or interpret these rules as needed to protect the server and the community.

!!! info "Official rules document"
    This page mirrors Townify's official rules. The authoritative, always-current version lives here: **[Townify Server Rules (Google Doc)](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YOt9tf9FxzV93Trj2E4JNTnDjW_D4fPdZ6XZFjzGWYk/edit?tab=t.0)**. If anything here ever differs from that document, the document wins.

## Chat & Community

- Do not harass, threaten, abuse, or target other players.
- Do not spam, advertise, flood chat, use excessive caps, abuse formatting, or otherwise make chat harder to read.
- **English is required in public server chats** so staff can moderate fairly.
- Hate speech, discrimination, slurs, and derogatory language are not allowed.
- Sexual content, graphic gore, drug-related content, extremist imagery, abuse, and exploitative depictions of minors are not allowed.
- Keep public chat appropriate. Avoid excessive profanity, sexual topics, politics, polarizing topics, and personal trauma discussions in public spaces.
- Do not dox players or share private personal information — real names, addresses, locations, phone numbers, emails, social media accounts, school or workplace information, IP addresses, photos, or any other identifying information — without clear permission.
- Do not impersonate staff, other players, or official server messages.
- Do not misuse the ticket system. Tickets must be used honestly and for their intended purpose. Spam tickets, false reports, harassment, trolling, ban evasion, or repeatedly reopening tickets after staff have given a final answer are not allowed.
- Do not intentionally make other players uncomfortable. If someone asks you to stop a topic, joke, comment, or behavior directed at them, stop and move on.

## Gameplay

- Do not use hacked clients, x-ray, cheat mods, unapproved scripts, cracked clients, or any other tool that gives you an unfair advantage.
- **Alternate accounts are not allowed.** Each player may only use one Minecraft account on the server.
- Do not use VPNs, proxies, alternate connections, or other methods to bypass punishments, limits, detection systems, or server restrictions.
- Do not exploit bugs, glitches, economy mistakes, broken features, or unintended mechanics. **Report them to staff.**
- Do not teach, share, sell, or spread exploits to other players.
- **Real-money trading is not allowed.** Do not buy, sell, or trade in-game items, currency, services, towns, claims, or accounts for real-world money or value.
- All written agreements are binding when they are clear and provable — including agreements made in chat, private messages, Discord, signs, books, or other written messages.
- Do not scam in trades, shops, auctions, services, rentals, loans, or any other transaction.
- Do not misrepresent items, prices, permissions, claims, services, or agreements.
- Do not exploit shop, auction, bank, currency, or other economy systems in unintended ways. Normal player-driven pricing, buying, selling, and market competition are allowed.
- Do not teleport kill, trap kill, bait players into PvP, mislead players into danger, or abuse teleport requests.
- Do not use inappropriate, inflammatory, misleading, or rule-breaking names for items, nicknames, towns, nations, claims, shops, warps, or other public labels.

## Automation & Macros

- Fully automatic machines or setups that generate money, items, drops, experience, or other value **without player interaction** are not allowed unless staff has explicitly approved that mechanic.
- Common utility machines such as smelters, brewing setups, and simple storage systems are allowed if they do not bypass server limits, automate profit, or run unattended in a harmful way.
- Bypassing AFK systems to keep farms, machines, macros, or accounts running is not allowed.
- Autoclickers, macros, and similar tools may not be used while AFK.
- Any allowed autoclicker or macro must stay **at or below 8 CPS**, must not automate full gameplay, and you must be able to respond to staff **within 2 minutes**.

## Claims, Towns & Griefing

- Do not break, place, alter, claim, or take blocks from another player's build, town, claim, or protected area without permission.
- Do not grief, deface, steal from, or intentionally damage claimed land, public builds, roads, farms, or server areas.
- Resource gathering should be done in **designated resource areas** when available. Do not strip-mine, deface, or mass-harvest the main world in a way that harms the server map or nearby players.
- Stealing from claimed land is not allowed, even if containers are unlocked or protections are missing.
- Do not abuse town, nation, claim, shop, or permission access to steal, sabotage, delete, disband, or damage another player's property, money, town, or nation.
- Having access to a claim, plot, container, town rank, or permission does **not** give you permission to steal, grief, sabotage, or misuse it.
- Abandoned, unclaimed settlements may be raided **only** for loot that is already accessible without breaking blocks, bypassing protections, or damaging the build. They may not be griefed, defaced, stripped for blocks, or destroyed.
- Do not build, claim, or destroy near another town or major build in a way that blocks expansion, traps players, starts disputes, or is clearly malicious.
- Do not create nuisance builds, claims, walls, traps, signs, or other structures meant to harass, block, intimidate, or interfere with another player, town, or build.
- Mayors and town staff must give a player **at least 72 hours' notice** before removing that player's build from a claimed town plot, unless staff approves an exception.
- Grief reports should be made by the owner of the affected town, claim, or build. If the owner has been offline for **7 or more days**, another affected player may report it.
- Staff may move, roll back, restore, or remove builds when needed to resolve grief, claim disputes, inactive builds, or server map issues.

!!! tip "Conflict between towns has an official system"
    Organized warfare between towns happens **only** through the [Siege War](../towns/war.md) system — which fights for control, not destruction. Working around town protection to raid or grief outside that system breaks the rules above.

## Staff Rulings & Appeals

- Staff have **final discretion** in unclear, incomplete, or disputed situations.
- Staff may remove or punish players whose behavior harms the server, the economy, the map, or the community, even if the behavior is not listed word-for-word here.
- If you believe you were punished unfairly, **appeal through the ticket system**.
- If a real-life emergency prevents you from maintaining a town, nation, claim, or other time-sensitive responsibility, **contact staff as soon as possible**.

!!! note "Key timeframes to remember"
    - **8 CPS** — the maximum click rate for any allowed autoclicker or macro
    - **2 minutes** — how quickly you must be able to respond to staff while using an allowed macro
    - **72 hours** — notice a mayor must give before removing your build from a town plot
    - **7 days** — how long a build owner must be offline before another affected player can file a grief report

Questions about a rule, or need to report something? Open a **ticket** or ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/townifymc). When in doubt, ask staff before acting.
