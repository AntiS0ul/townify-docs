# Economy & Currencies

TownifyMC runs on **two currencies**, each earned and spent differently. Knowing what each one is for saves you a lot of confusion.

## The two currencies

| Currency | Symbol | What it's for | How you earn it |
|----------|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Money** | <span class="chip money">$</span> | The main economy — ranking up, shops, town costs, claims | Jobs, selling items, player trade |
| **Gems** | <span class="chip gems">Gems</span> | [Gem Store](../perks/store.md), top-tier upgrades | Chat games, fishing comps, crates, events, [online store](https://townify.tebex.io/category/gems) |

### :material-cash: Money (`$`)

This is the currency that runs everything day-to-day. The entire [rank ladder](ranks.md) is priced in money, town and claim costs are in money, and most shops trade in money.

```
/bal            # check your Money AND Gems balance
/baltop         # see the richest players
/pay <player> <amount>   # send money to someone
```

**Where money comes from:**

- [**Jobs**](jobs.md) — your primary, reliable income
- Selling goods on the [player markets and auction house](../money/shops.md)
- [Fishing competitions](../money/fishing.md)
- [Daily quests](quests.md) and [voting](../rewards/voting.md) payouts

### :material-diamond-stone: Gems

Gems are a **premium currency**, rarer than money and used for higher-value purchases.

**Earning Gems in game:**

- [**Chat Games**](../rewards/events.md#chat-games): win 3–50 Gems for answering first
- [**Fishing competitions**](../money/fishing.md): top finishers win Gems
- **Crates and events**

You can also buy Gems on the [online store](https://townify.tebex.io/category/gems) (`/store`).

**Spending Gems:**

- The [**Gem Store**](../perks/store.md) (`/servershop`): crate keys, chunk loaders, job boost tokens, Town Flight, the premium Battle Pass, and lifetime donor ranks
- Top-tier **home and player-warp upgrades** in `/upgrades`

```
/bal            # shows Gems alongside your money
/servershop     # spend Gems
```

## Smart money tips for new players

1. **Don't hoard early.** Spend your first earnings on ranking up — early ranks are cheap and unlock tools that make earning faster.
2. **Vote every day.** It's free money and crate keys for ~1 minute of effort.
3. **Pick complementary jobs.** Once you unlock a [2nd and 3rd job slot](jobs.md), choose jobs that reward the same activities so a single play session pays multiple ways.
4. **Watch the market.** It's a player-run economy, so prices move. Buy low, sell high, and undercut other sellers where you can.
5. **The ladder gets steep fast.** Ranks 1–10 are reachable through normal play; ranks 20+ run into the tens of millions. That's intentional endgame content — don't be discouraged, just settle in.
